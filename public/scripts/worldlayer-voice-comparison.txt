From above, a city can look almost inevitable. Roads follow the shoreline, rail lines cross the valley, and neighborhoods gather around the places where people can move. But every line on a map began as a choice, shaped by terrain, technology, and time. Look closer, and the familiar pattern becomes a record of tradeoffs: where a bridge was built, where a river was left alone, and where a new route changed everything. Worldlayer follows those choices across geography, infrastructure, and science, asking a simple question: how did this place become what it is today?
