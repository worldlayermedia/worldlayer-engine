Every journey begins with a simple point on the globe. From this height, borders fade and the shape of the planet becomes clear. We turn toward Canada, a vast landscape of water, forests, roads, and cities. The camera draws closer, revealing patterns that were invisible from far above. Toronto comes into view beside Lake Ontario. Streets form a precise grid, while neighborhoods spread in every direction. Now we orbit the city, slide across its skyline, and move closer to the ground. Each movement changes what we notice. Worldlayer connects these views into one continuous story, giving the landscape time to speak for itself.
